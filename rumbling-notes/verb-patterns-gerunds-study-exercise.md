# Exercise: Verbs Followed by Gerunds

Complete the sentences using the gerund (-ing form) of the verb in parentheses.

1.  He admitted _________ (take) the last piece of cake.
2.  I try to avoid _________ (drive) during rush hour.
3.  Have you considered _________ (study) abroad next year?
4.  She denied _________ (break) the window.
5.  We really enjoy _________ (watch) old movies on Sundays.
6.  Please finish _________ (eat) your dinner before you play.
7.  Can you imagine _________ (live) in a world without internet?
8.  Keep _________ (try), and you will eventually succeed.
9.  I miss _________ (go) to the gym regularly.
10. The teacher suggested _________ (read) an extra book for the assignment.

---

## Answer Key

1.  taking
2.  driving
3.  studying
4.  breaking
5.  watching
6.  eating
7.  living
8.  trying
9.  going
10. reading

## Your Personal Gotchas & Key Reminders (from practice!)

*   **Verb: `consider` + Gerund vs. `that` clause:**
    *   `Consider` is followed by the **-ing form (gerund)** when the action directly follows the verb (e.g., *"Have you considered studying abroad?"*).
    *   When the gerund has a *different subject* from the main verb, use the **possessive form** before the gerund for formal/correct English:
        *   **Correct:** *"Do you consider **our having covered** all topics?"* (Not: *"Do you consider we having covered..."*)
        *   **Correct:** *"I appreciate **his helping** me."* (Not: *"I appreciate him helping me."*)
    *   Alternatively, for a full clause, use **`that`**: *"Do you consider **that we have covered** all topics?"*

# Exercise: Possessive Forms with Gerunds

Complete the following sentences, choosing the most appropriate and grammatically correct option. Pay close attention to whether a possessive form is needed before the gerund.

1.  Do you consider _________ (they/their) applying for the scholarship a good idea?
2.  The manager insisted on _________ (us/our) reviewing the report before the meeting.
3.  I don't mind _________ (him/his) disagreeing, but he should do it respectfully.
4.  She couldn't believe _________ (the child/the child's) telling such a blatant lie.
5.  We regret _________ (not inform/not informing/our not informing) you sooner about the change. (Choose the best phrase)
6.  My parents appreciate _________ (me/my) visiting them regularly.
7.  Do you consider _________ (the project/the project's) being completed on time to be crucial?
8.  I object to _________ (you/your) interrupting me constantly.
9.  Is there any chance of _________ (we/our) getting a discount?
10. The teacher approved of _________ (Sarah/Sarah's) using the dictionary during the test.

---

## Answer Key for Possessive Forms with Gerunds

1.  their
2.  our
3.  his
4.  the child's
5.  our not informing
6.  my
7.  the project's
8.  your
9.  our
10. Sarah's
