# Exercise: Verbs Followed by Gerunds

Complete the sentences using the gerund (-ing form) of the verb in parentheses.

1.  He admitted _________ (take) the last piece of cake.
2.  I try to avoid _________ (drive) during rush hour.
3.  Have you considered _________ (study) abroad next year?
4.  She denied _________ (break) the window.
5.  We really enjoy _________ (watch) old movies on Sundays.
6.  Please finish _________ (eat) your dinner before you play.
7.  Can you imagine _________ (live) in a world without internet?
8.  Keep _________ (try), and you will eventually succeed.
9.  I miss _________ (go) to the gym regularly.
10. The teacher suggested _________ (read) an extra book for the assignment.

---

## Answer Key

1.  taking
2.  driving
3.  studying
4.  breaking
5.  watching
6.  eating
7.  living
8.  trying
9.  going
10. reading
