# Causative Verbs and Verbs with Object + Infinitive - Study Exercises (B2 Level)

This file contains exercises to practice the causative verbs and verbs followed by an object and infinitive discussed in `rumbling-notes/causative-and-object-infinitives.md`.

---

**Part 1: Choose the correct option.**

1.  My boss _______ (made/got) me stay late to finish the project.
2.  I need to _______ (get/make) my hair cut this weekend.
3.  She wouldn't _______ (let/allow) her children watch that movie.
4.  The principal _______ (ordered/made) the students to apologize.
5.  We've _______ (have got to/must) buy some food; the fridge is empty!
6.  The government _______ (made/allowed) the new law to be passed.
7.  Could you please _______ (get/make) your brother to help me with these bags?
8.  I felt really tired, so my friend _______ (let/allowed) me go home early.
9.  He _______ (has got to/has to) work extra hours because of the new contract.
10. The teacher _______ (made/got) us write a long essay.

---

**Part 2: Complete the sentences using the correct form of the verb in brackets.**

1.  My manager always _______ (make) me _______ (check) my work twice.
2.  Can you _______ (get) the plumber _______ (come) tomorrow morning?
3.  They wouldn't _______ (let) us _______ (take) photos inside the museum.
4.  The judge _______ (order) the witness _______ (tell) the truth.
5.  I've _______ (have got) _______ (study) for my exam next week.
6.  Are you _______ (allow) _______ (use) your phone in class? (passive form)
7.  She _______ (get) her car _______ (service) last week. (causative with thing)
8.  We were _______ (make) _______ (wait) for hours in the queue. (passive form of make)
9.  My parents _______ (let) me _______ (choose) my own university.
10. I finally _______ (get) him _______ (understand) my point of view.

4.  She got me helping with the presentation.
