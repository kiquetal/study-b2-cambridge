# Modals - Study Exercises (B2 Level)

This file contains exercises to practice the modals discussed in `8_modals.md`.

---**Exercises**---

**Part 1: Choose the correct modal verb.**

1.  You _______ (can/must) be joking! That's incredible!
2.  When I was younger, I _______ (could/can) run much faster than I do now.
3.  She _______ (must/might) be home already; it's almost 8 PM.
4.  I _______ (have to/should) finish this report by tomorrow morning. My boss insists.
5.  You look tired. You _______ (ought to/must) get some rest.
6.  _______ (Could/May) you please help me with this heavy box?
7.  He _______ (can't/might not) be at the conference; he emailed me this morning saying he was sick.
8.  We _______ (should/must) really try that new restaurant everyone is talking about.
9.  She _______ (can/be able to) play the guitar very well, even though she only started last year.
10. Children _______ (must/should) always be polite to their elders.

---

**Part 2: Complete the sentences using the correct form of the modals in brackets (present or past).**

1.  I wish I _______ (study) harder for the test last week.
2.  She said she _______ (be able to) join us for dinner tonight.
3.  He looks very pale. He _______ (might/have) fainted.
4.  You _______ (not/have) to wear a tie for the interview, but it would make a good impression.
5.  If I had known you were coming, I _______ (bake) a cake.
6.  You _______ (should/not/say) such a rude thing to her. It was uncalled for.
7.  He _______ (must/have) a lot of money; he just bought a new sports car.
8.  I _______ (could/go) to the concert, but I didn't have anyone to go with.
9.  They _______ (ought to/help) us with the move, but they never showed up.
10. I _______ (can/understand) what you mean, but I don't agree.

---

**Part 3: Error Correction**

Find and correct the mistake in each sentence.

1.  If I would have known about the party, I would have come.
2.  She can to sing beautifully.
3.  You must to finish your homework before watching TV.
4.  He might be at home now, but I am not sure.
5.  I could not go to the cinema yesterday because I had to work.

**Nice to Know:**
*   'Ought to' is less common than 'should' and is mainly used for advice or to express what is morally right.
*   When using modals for past events, remember the structure: modal + have + past participle.
